# Showzy: product brief for the logo designer

Showzy is the new name for **Naada**. This document describes what the product is, who it is for and where the logo will be used. It contains no design direction on purpose.

## 1. Summary

Showzy is a web platform for **live arts events in Mysuru and Bengaluru** (Karnataka, India). It does two jobs:

1. **Ticketing and discovery.** People browse events near them, buy tickets and get a QR code on their phone to enter.
2. **Booking.** Venues and artists find each other, negotiate a fee in-app and confirm bookings with payment held until after the performance.

It is built for small, grassroots events: open mics, jam nights, pub gigs, classical evenings, small theatre, comedy and poetry nights. It is not aimed at stadium concerts.

## 2. Domain

- **Industry:** live events, ticketing and talent booking (an events marketplace).
- **Art forms covered:** music, dance, theatre, poetry, comedy. Music is the largest today, and the product is meant to cover live arts generally.
- **Geography:** Mysuru and Bengaluru first. Currency is Indian rupees (₹). Languages: English now, Kannada planned (the interface already supports Kannada script, but the switch is hidden until translations are reviewed).
- **Platform:** website, mobile-first (most users are on phones), installable as a home-screen app. No native app yet.
- **Stage:** early. A two-person team, pre-launch, with a first live test planned around New Year's Eve 2026 events.

## 3. Who uses it

| User | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | Discover events by city and art form, buy tickets, show a QR code at the door, keep tickets on their phone. |
| **Hosts** (pubs, cafés, studios, auditoriums, community spaces) | List events for free, set ticket tiers, see sales, scan tickets at the door, search and book artists, pay for bookings. |
| **Artists** (bands, solo musicians, dancers, theatre groups, poets, comedians) | Build a profile, receive booking offers, negotiate, get paid after the performance. |
| **Admin** (the Showzy team) | Verify users, review disputes and ledger, moderate messages. |

## 4. How it works

- **Buying a ticket:** pick an event, choose a tier, pay online (cards, UPI), receive a QR ticket. Free events need no payment.
- **At the door:** the host scans the QR code with a phone.
- **Booking an artist:** host sends an offer, both sides can counter (up to three rounds), the host pays into escrow, the artist performs, and the money is released after the event (72 hours unless there is a dispute).
- **Links travel through WhatsApp groups**, so shared event links and their preview cards matter.

## 5. Business model (stated honestly in the product)

- Ticket buyers pay a booking fee of ₹19 per paid ticket. Free events carry no fee.
- Artists are not charged. The venue pays Showzy 5% on top of the agreed artist fee, and the artist receives the agreed fee less 1% TDS (a statutory tax deduction).
- Listing an event is free for hosts.

## 6. Name

- **Showzy**: pronounced "show-zee".
- The previous name, **Naada**, is retired. The old logo was a set of vertical bars with a red centre bar, and a wordmark "NAADA" with the letter D in a different colour. The new identity is separate from it.
- Trademark, domain and app-store availability for "Showzy" have not yet been checked.
- Tagline candidates, none chosen: "Live nights, near you." and "Built by artists. For the live arts community."

## 7. Existing brand facts (for reference, not instructions)

These are what the product uses today. The designer may keep or change them.

- **Colours:** dark background `#120E0B`, cream text `#F3E9D6`, red accent `#C4472E`, gold `#E8B85C`. Light mode: background `#F3F0EB`, text `#1B1713`, accent `#B23A22`.
- **Fonts:** Josefin Sans (hero headlines), Megrim (section titles), Manrope (body text), JetBrains Mono (prices and codes).
- **Interface:** dark by default with a light mode, 4 px corner radius, borders instead of shadows, no stock photography (posters are typeset from the event name).

## 8. Preferences the founders have stated

- It must not look AI-generated.
- It must not look cartoonish.

## 9. Where the logo will be used

| Placement | Size / note |
|---|---|
| Browser tab icon | 16 and 32 px |
| Phone home-screen icon | 180 px (iOS), 192 and 512 px, plus a maskable 512 px Android version with padding |
| Website header | wordmark about 19–24 px tall on a translucent bar, next to a city picker and search |
| Link share card (WhatsApp, social) | 1200 × 630 px |
| Ticket stub / QR ticket | small, one colour |
| Printed venue posters and table cards | one-colour version |
| Social profile picture | circular crop |
| Loading screen | centred, on a dark background |

## 10. Deliverables needed

- Wordmark, symbol and a combined lockup (horizontal and stacked).
- Versions for dark backgrounds, light backgrounds and single colour.
- SVG files with text outlined, plus PNG exports at 16, 32, 48, 180, 192 and 512 px and the 1200 × 630 share card.
- Clear-space and minimum-size rules.
